Privacy Policy

Hibol Minesweeper · Last updated: September 27, 2026

Hibol Minesweeper is a free game made by an independent developer (“I”, “me”). This page explains what data the game uses, why, and how you can delete it. The short version: your progress stays on your device, and the only thing sent online is what the leaderboards need.

Data that stays on your device

Your progress, settings, run history, achievements and in-game items are stored locally on your device. They are never sent to me. If you use “Export save”, the file is created on your device and you decide what to do with it.

Data sent to the leaderboard server

To show online leaderboards, the game sends:

  • a random player ID, generated on your device (it is not linked to your name, email, phone or Google account);
  • the username you choose (shown publicly on the leaderboards);
  • for Legacy games: your best time per difficulty and the game seed. The moves of a winning game are also sent so the server can check the result; they are used for that check only and are not stored;
  • for Infinite runs: distance, cells revealed, mines, hearts and robots triggered, and whether machines were used;
  • the dates when these were created or last updated.

This data is used only to run the leaderboards. Your username and results are public; your player ID is not shown to anyone.

What the game does not do

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • No account, email address or password is required.
  • Your data is never sold or shared with third parties.

Where the data is stored

The leaderboard server is a server I rent from Hetzner. All traffic between the game and the server is encrypted (HTTPS). The server does not keep access logs. Its error logs can occasionally include the IP address of a request that failed; they are used only to fix problems and are automatically deleted as new logs replace them. The web version of the game is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical data under its own privacy statement.

Deleting your data

Your online data is kept until you delete it. To delete your username and all your leaderboard results:

  • In the game: open the menu, then About, then “Delete online data”. Deletion is immediate. Your local progress on the device is kept.
  • Without the game: email hibol18@gmail.com with your username. I will delete the matching data within 30 days.

Uninstalling the game removes the local data from your device, but not your online leaderboard entries: use one of the options above for that.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. For any question, write to hibol18@gmail.com.
